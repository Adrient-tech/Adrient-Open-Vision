OpenVision live endpoint

This is the WebSocket endpoint of the realtime broadcast server, protocol adrient.openvision/live@1. The 3D viewer connects here automatically — open the Live Instrument and press Go live.

Direct connection: ws://host:8081/live. Server sends hello, frame, ping; client may send {"type":"resume","after":<last frame_id>} to replay missed frames and pong.

Start the server: node server/live.mjs --port 8081 --replay viewer/scene.json (or --tail world_state.jsonl 10 for a live feed).
